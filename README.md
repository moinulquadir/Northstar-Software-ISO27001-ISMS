# Northstar Software Labs — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS Portfolio Project

## Portfolio Scenario
Northstar Software Labs is a fictional 180-person B2B SaaS company headquartered in New York with a remote workforce and an AWS-hosted workflow platform.

This repository demonstrates an end-to-end ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System (ISMS) implementation exercise, including governance, scope, asset management, risk assessment and treatment, Statement of Applicability, policies, procedures, operational records, internal audit, corrective actions, management review, and certification readiness.

> **Important:** Northstar Software Labs, its assets, incidents, suppliers, evidence, audit results, and metrics are fictional/simulated for a cybersecurity GRC portfolio. This repository is not evidence that any organization is ISO 27001 certified.

## ISMS Lifecycle
1. Initiation and business context
2. ISMS scope and boundaries
3. Asset and information inventory
4. Risk assessment
5. Risk treatment and acceptance
6. Statement of Applicability
7. Control implementation
8. Evidence and operational records
9. Internal audit
10. Corrective and preventive action (CAPA)
11. Management review
12. Certification readiness and continual improvement

## Framework Alignment
The project is designed around ISO/IEC 27001:2022 concepts and Annex A control themes. It demonstrates how a GRC practitioner can connect risks to controls, controls to policies/procedures, and procedures to operational evidence.

## Working Principle
The project intentionally includes realistic imperfections, findings, exceptions, corrective actions, and simulated test results rather than presenting a fictional organization as having perfect controls.
