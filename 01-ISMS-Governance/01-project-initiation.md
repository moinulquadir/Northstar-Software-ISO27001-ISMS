# ISMS Project Initiation

**Organization:** Northstar Software Labs (fictional)  
**Project:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS implementation  
**Business:** B2B workflow SaaS, 180 employees, AWS-hosted, hybrid/remote workforce

## Objectives
Establish a risk-based ISMS covering governance, customer information, SaaS operations, secure development, corporate IT, suppliers, and continuity. Produce auditable policies, procedures, records, and improvement actions.

## Stakeholders
Executive Sponsor — COO; ISMS Owner — Information Security Manager; CTO — technology controls; Engineering Manager — SDLC; IT Manager — endpoints/IAM; Operations Manager — continuity; Privacy Officer — personal information; Internal Auditor — independent assurance.

## Success Criteria
Approved scope and policies; current asset/risk registers; SoA; operating procedures; evidence records; completed internal audit; CAPA; management review; certification-readiness assessment.
