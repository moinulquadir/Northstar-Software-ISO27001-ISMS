# ISMS Scope and Context

## Scope Statement
The ISMS covers people, processes, information, applications, AWS infrastructure, endpoints, corporate IT, customer support, secure SDLC, security operations, critical suppliers, the New York office, and remote-work environments used to design, develop, operate, support, or administer the Northstar B2B SaaS platform.

## Interfaces / Exclusions
Customer-owned networks and endpoints are outside direct operational control but relevant interfaces are risk-managed. Supplier internal operations are outside Northstar's boundary; supplier security is managed through contracts, due diligence, monitoring, and continuity requirements.

## Context
Key interested parties include customers, employees, leadership, regulators where applicable, suppliers, and business partners. Key issues include cloud dependency, remote work, customer trust, privacy obligations, software supply-chain risk, availability expectations, and changing threat conditions.
