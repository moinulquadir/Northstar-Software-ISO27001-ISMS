# Records Retention and Secure Disposal Policy

**Document ID:** P-018 | **Owner:** Compliance Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Business and security records shall be retained for periods defined by legal, contractual, operational, and evidentiary requirements. Records subject to legal hold shall not be destroyed. Disposal shall prevent unauthorized recovery and shall be documented when evidence is required. Owners shall periodically review retention schedules for relevance.
