# Remote Work and Endpoint Security Policy

**Document ID:** P-008 | **Owner:** IT Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Company-managed endpoints shall use supported operating systems, encryption, screen locking, endpoint protection, centralized management, and timely security updates. Remote access shall use approved secure channels and MFA. Personnel shall not store Restricted information on unmanaged personal devices unless specifically approved. Lost or stolen devices must be reported promptly for containment.
