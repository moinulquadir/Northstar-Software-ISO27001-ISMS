# Logging and Monitoring Policy

**Document ID:** P-014 | **Owner:** Security Operations Lead | **Approver:** CTO | **Version:** 1.0

Security-relevant events shall be logged for systems based on risk and operational need. Logs shall be protected against unauthorized alteration, retained for defined periods, and reviewed through monitoring and alerting processes. Time synchronization shall be maintained for relevant systems. Access to logs shall be restricted and investigation records shall preserve sufficient context for incident response and audit.
