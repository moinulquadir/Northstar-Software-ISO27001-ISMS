# Authentication and Password Policy

**Document ID:** P-006 | **Owner:** IT Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Authentication shall use centrally managed identities where practical. MFA is required for privileged, remote, and high-risk access. Passwords must be unique, protected from disclosure, and managed through approved password-management mechanisms. Default credentials shall be changed before deployment. Service credentials shall be stored in approved secrets-management solutions and rotated according to risk.
