# Backup and Recovery Policy

**Document ID:** P-015 | **Owner:** Cloud Operations Lead | **Approver:** CTO | **Version:** 1.0

Critical information and system configurations shall be backed up according to defined recovery requirements. Backups shall be access-controlled and protected from unauthorized deletion or modification. Restore tests shall be performed periodically and results recorded. Failed tests require corrective action. Backup retention shall reflect contractual, legal, operational, and risk requirements.
