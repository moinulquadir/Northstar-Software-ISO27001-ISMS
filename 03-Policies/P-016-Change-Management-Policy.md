# Change Management Policy

**Document ID:** P-016 | **Owner:** Engineering Manager | **Approver:** CTO | **Version:** 1.0

Production changes shall be requested, risk-assessed, reviewed, approved, tested, implemented, and documented. Emergency changes may follow an expedited path but require retrospective review. Changes shall have rollback or recovery considerations where practical. Unauthorized changes shall be investigated. Change records must identify requester, approver, implementation date, affected service, and outcome.
