# Security Awareness Policy

**Document ID:** P-019 | **Owner:** Information Security Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Personnel shall complete security awareness training during onboarding and periodically thereafter. Training shall address phishing, credential protection, data handling, incident reporting, acceptable use, remote work, and role-specific risks. Completion shall be tracked. High-risk roles receive additional training. Management shall review training results and recurring weaknesses.
