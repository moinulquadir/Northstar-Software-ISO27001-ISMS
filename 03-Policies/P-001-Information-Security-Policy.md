# Information Security Policy

**Document ID:** P-001 | **Company:** Northstar Software Labs (fictional) | **Version:** 1.0 | **Owner:** Information Security Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Classification:** Internal

> Portfolio simulation aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022. Northstar is fictional and this document is not evidence of certification.

## Purpose
Set management direction and minimum requirements for protecting company, customer, and service information.

## Requirements
- Maintain an approved ISMS and measurable security objectives.
- Assess and treat information-security risks using the approved methodology.
- Assign security responsibilities to accountable owners.
- Provide role-appropriate security awareness training.
- Report, investigate, contain, and learn from security incidents.
- Review the policy at least annually and after material change.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented justification, risk assessment, compensating controls where practical, an owner, expiry/review date, and approval by the designated risk authority.

## Evidence
Policy approvals, risk records, training records, incident tickets, audit results, and management-review records.
