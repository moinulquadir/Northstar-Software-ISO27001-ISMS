# Access Control Policy

**Document ID:** P-002 | **Owner:** Information Security Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0 | **Classification:** Internal

Access shall be based on business need, least privilege, and role requirements. Unique identities are required; shared accounts require approved exceptions. MFA is required for administrative, remote, and other risk-sensitive access. Access must be approved before provisioning and removed promptly on termination or role change. Privileged access and critical-system access are reviewed quarterly.

**Evidence:** access requests, IAM records, joiner/mover/leaver tickets, quarterly reviews, privileged-access reviews, and exception records.
