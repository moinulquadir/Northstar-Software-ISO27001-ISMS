# Incident Response Policy

**Document ID:** P-010 | **Owner:** Information Security Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Suspected security incidents shall be reported immediately through the approved channel. The response process covers identification, triage, containment, eradication, recovery, communications, evidence preservation, lessons learned, and corrective action. Severity determines escalation and notification requirements. Incident records shall document decisions, timestamps, owners, evidence, and closure criteria.
