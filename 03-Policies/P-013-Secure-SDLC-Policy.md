# Secure SDLC Policy

**Document ID:** P-013 | **Owner:** Engineering Manager | **Approver:** CTO | **Version:** 1.0

Security shall be integrated into requirements, design, coding, review, testing, release, and maintenance. Developers shall use approved source-control workflows and peer review. Security testing shall address applicable application and dependency risks. Production releases require evidence of required reviews and approval. Security defects shall be tracked through the engineering workflow.
