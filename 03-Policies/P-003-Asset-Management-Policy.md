# Asset Management Policy

**Document ID:** P-003 | **Owner:** IT Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Northstar shall maintain an accurate inventory of information, applications, endpoints, cloud resources, services, and other assets supporting the ISMS. Each asset shall have an owner, classification, criticality, lifecycle status, and review frequency. New assets shall be registered before production use where practical. Retired assets shall be securely disposed of or returned to the supplier, with records retained.

**Evidence:** asset register, ownership assignments, onboarding records, disposal records, and periodic inventory reviews.
