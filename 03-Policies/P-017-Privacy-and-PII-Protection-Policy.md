# Privacy and PII Protection Policy

**Document ID:** P-017 | **Owner:** Privacy Officer | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Personal information shall be processed only for authorized purposes and according to applicable law, contracts, and documented privacy requirements. Collection and access shall be limited to business need. Retention, disclosure, data-subject requests, incident handling, and deletion shall follow approved processes. Privacy risks shall be considered during new products, integrations, and material changes.
