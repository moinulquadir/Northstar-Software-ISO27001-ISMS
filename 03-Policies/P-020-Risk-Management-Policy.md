# Risk Management Policy

**Document ID:** P-020 | **Owner:** Information Security Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Northstar shall maintain a repeatable process to identify, analyze, evaluate, treat, accept, monitor, and communicate information-security risks. Risk owners are accountable for treatment decisions. Treatment may include mitigation, avoidance, transfer, or acceptance. Residual risk shall be documented after treatment. The risk register shall be reviewed at least quarterly and after material change.
