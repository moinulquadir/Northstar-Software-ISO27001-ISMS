# Cryptography Policy

**Document ID:** P-007 | **Owner:** Security Engineering Lead | **Approver:** CTO | **Version:** 1.0

Approved cryptographic mechanisms shall protect Confidential and Restricted information in transit and at rest where risk or contractual requirements warrant. Keys shall be generated, stored, rotated, revoked, and recovered through approved key-management services. Weak or obsolete algorithms shall not be introduced without documented risk acceptance and compensating controls. Cryptographic configuration shall be reviewed when systems change.
