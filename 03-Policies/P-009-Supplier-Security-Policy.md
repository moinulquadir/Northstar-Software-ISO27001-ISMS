# Supplier Security Policy

**Document ID:** P-009 | **Owner:** Vendor Management Lead | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Security requirements shall be considered before onboarding suppliers that process company or customer information or support critical services. Risk-based due diligence may include security questionnaires, independent assurance reports, contract clauses, breach notification terms, access restrictions, and continuity requirements. Critical suppliers shall be reviewed periodically and on material change.
