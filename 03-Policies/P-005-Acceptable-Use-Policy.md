# Acceptable Use Policy

**Document ID:** P-005 | **Owner:** IT Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Company systems are provided for authorized business use. Personnel shall protect credentials, avoid unauthorized software and services, use approved storage and collaboration tools, protect company devices, and report suspected phishing, malware, loss, or misuse. Prohibited activities include unauthorized access, security testing without authorization, deliberate circumvention of controls, and unlawful use. Violations may result in corrective or disciplinary action.
