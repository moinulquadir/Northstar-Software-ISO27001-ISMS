# Data Classification and Handling Policy

**Document ID:** P-004 | **Owner:** Information Security Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Information shall be classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted according to business impact and sensitivity. Owners determine handling requirements. Confidential and Restricted information shall use approved storage, transmission, access, and disposal methods. Customer data shall be handled according to contract and applicable privacy obligations. Classification shall be reviewed when use or sensitivity changes.

**Evidence:** data inventories, labels, access reviews, encryption configuration, disposal records, and training.
