# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy

**Document ID:** P-011 | **Owner:** Operations Manager | **Approver:** COO | **Version:** 1.0

Northstar shall maintain continuity and recovery arrangements for critical services based on business impact and risk. Recovery objectives shall be defined for critical systems. Backups shall be protected and periodically restored to verify usability. Disaster-recovery exercises shall test technical recovery, dependencies, communications, and decision-making, with corrective actions tracked to closure.
