# Supplier Security Assessment Questionnaire

1. What security certifications or independent assurance reports are available?
2. How is customer data segregated?
3. What encryption is used in transit and at rest?
4. How is privileged access controlled and reviewed?
5. What is the incident notification commitment?
6. How are vulnerabilities identified and remediated?
7. What continuity and disaster-recovery capabilities exist?
8. Where is customer data processed?
9. How are subcontractors managed?
10. How is data returned or deleted at contract termination?

Assessment outcome must include risk rating, required contract clauses, remediation actions, owner, and review date.
