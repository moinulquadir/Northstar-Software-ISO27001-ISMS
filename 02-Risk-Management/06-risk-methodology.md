# Risk Assessment Methodology

Likelihood and impact are scored 1–5. Inherent risk = likelihood × impact. Scores 1–4 are Low, 5–9 Moderate, 10–16 High, and 17–25 Critical. Risk owners evaluate existing controls, determine treatment, and reassess residual risk after treatment.

Treatment options: mitigate, avoid, transfer, or accept. Acceptance requires authority appropriate to the residual-risk level and an expiry/review date. Risks are reviewed quarterly and when significant business, technology, supplier, legal, or threat changes occur.
